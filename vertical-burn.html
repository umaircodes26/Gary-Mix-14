AVIATION MATERIALS TESTING

60s & 12s Vertical Burn

EASA/FAA 25.853(a), Appendix F Part I(a)(1)(i) and (ii)

Two vertical burn durations for materials used in aircraft interiors. Each duration has its own exposure time and acceptance limits.

ENQUIRE NOW REQUEST A QUOTE

Sample
Requirements

At least 3 specimens per material/condition. Normally 2 × 12 in exposed area; use the actual sample where smaller.

Two durations, one vertical specimen setup.

Pass criteria

  • 60-second: average burn length ≤ 6 in
  • average afterflame ≤ 15 s
  • flaming drips after falling ≤ 3 s
  • 12-second: average burn length ≤ 8 in
  • average afterflame ≤ 15 s
  • flaming drips after falling ≤ 5 s
Two durations, one vertical specimen setup.
