Oil burner

Seat Cushion Combination

EASA/FAA 25.853(c), Appendix F Part II

A full seat-cushion oil burner test for the cushion and back assembly, assessing burn length and weight loss after burner exposure.

Enquire now Request a quote

Sample
Requirements

At least 3 sets. Bottom cushion: 457 × 508 × 102 mm (18 × 20 × 4 in); back cushion: 457 × 635 × 51 mm (18 × 25 × 2 in).

Pass criteria

  • At least two-thirds of specimens must not burn through to the opposite side and burn length must not exceed 17 in
  • Average weight loss ≤ 10%
  • At least two-thirds of each set ≤ 10%
Final test scope and acceptance criteria should be confirmed against the applicable regulation, amendment and certification basis.
