AVIATION MATERIALS TESTING

30s / 45° Burn Test

EASA/FAA 25.855(d), Appendix F Part I(a)(2)(ii) and (b)(6)

An inclined flame test used for insulation liners and panels to assess flame spread, penetration, afterflame and glow after a 30-second exposure.

Requirements

At least 3 specimens; exposed area at least 8 × 8 in.

Applicable to insulation liners and panels.

Pass criteria

  • No flame penetration
  • Average afterflame time ≤ 15 s
  • Average glow time ≤ 10 s
