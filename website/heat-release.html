Heat Release Test

EASA/FAA 25.853(d), Appendix F Part IV(e) and (g)

Measures heat release from aircraft interior panels using a controlled radiant heat source and pilot burner.

Enquire now Request a quote

Sample
Requirements

At least 3 specimens. Standard specimen is 150 × 150 mm; thickness represents the installed part, up to 45 mm.

For interior panels and other applicable cabin materials.

Pass criteria

  • Average total heat release during the first 2 minutes ≤ 65 kW·min/m²
  • Average peak heat release rate ≤ 65 kW/m²
Final test scope and acceptance criteria should be confirmed against the applicable regulation, amendment and certification basis.
