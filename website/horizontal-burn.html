15s Horizontal Burn Tests

EASA/FAA 25.853(a), Appendix F Part I(a)(1)(iv) and (v)

Horizontal flame propagation tests for aircraft interior materials. The applicable category depends on the material location and contribution to fire growth.

Sample
Requirements

At least 3 specimens. Normally 2 × 12 in exposed area, with a 10 in timed length and approximately 1.5 in pre-burn.

Two criteria under the 15-second horizontal method.

Pass criteria

  • Category (iv): average burn rate ≤ 2.5 in/min
  • Category (v): average burn rate ≤ 4 in/min; applies to specified small/non-contributing parts and excludes wires/cables.
Final test scope and acceptance criteria should be confirmed against the applicable regulation, amendment and certification basis.
